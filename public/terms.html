Who can use it

MFU Tracker is for MFU administrators, MFU coaches, and parents or guardians of students enrolled in MFU classes. Accounts are approved by MFU.

Your account

Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices. Tell MFU straight away if you think someone else has used your account.

What you add

Only add information, photos and videos you have the right to share, and only about your own children (parents) or the students you coach (coaches). Do not use MFU Tracker to contact families for anything other than MFU activities.

The service

MFU provides MFU Tracker free of charge to its families and coaches, as it is, and may change or stop it. MFU may suspend an account that is misused.
