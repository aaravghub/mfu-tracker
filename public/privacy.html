Privacy policy

MFU Tracker, run by Mallakhamb Federation USA (MFU). Last updated 13 November 2026.

What MFU Tracker is

MFU Tracker is the class-management tool MFU coaches and parents use for attendance, skill progress, fitness, evaluations, events and messages. It is used only by MFU staff, coaches and the families of enrolled students.

What we keep

Student names, dates of birth, gender, center and batch, attendance, skill levels and progress, fitness results, coach notes, evaluation results, and photos or videos coaches or parents add. Parent and coach names, email addresses, phone numbers, home address and emergency contact. Account passwords are stored only as secure one-way hashes.

How we use it

Only to run MFU classes: taking attendance, tracking progress, organising evaluations, competitions and demos, and sending families updates, invitations and the information they ask for. We do not sell or rent any information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Google account access

MFU Tracker connects to MFU's own Google account (automallakhamb@gmail.com) to send email to families and coaches (Gmail), create meeting invitations with Google Meet links (Google Calendar), and create the evaluation spreadsheets and class videos it stores in that account's Google Drive. It only works with files it created itself. Information from Google is used only for these features, and its use follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. MFU Tracker does not ask any parent or coach to connect their own Google account.

Who can see it

Parents see only their own children. Coaches and MFU administrators see the students they work with. Photos and videos are visible only inside MFU Tracker to people who are signed in, or to people given a video's link.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare (database and photo storage) and in MFU's Google account (email, calendar, spreadsheets and videos). Free AI services may be used to read a coach's typed or spoken class notes so they can be turned into updates; the coach reviews every change before it is saved.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep information while a student is enrolled with MFU. A parent can ask us to correct or delete their or their child's information at any time by emailing automallakhamb@gmail.com.

Children

Information about children is entered by their parents or by MFU coaches, only for running classes. Children do not sign in to MFU Tracker.
